About

I'm an Assistant Professor with over 15 years of teaching experience in Computer Science, currently based at ACE Engineering College, Ghatkesar. My academic focus is Cyber Security — I'm currently pursuing a Ph.D. in Cyber Security at KL University, Vijayawada, having completed my coursework in October 2025.

Over my career, I've taught subjects spanning cyber security and systems — including Cyber Crime Investigation & Digital Forensics, Network Security, Blockchain Technologies, and Python Programming — while working on research in machine learning and applied software systems.

Alongside teaching, I've taken on coordination and leadership roles, including Placements Coordinator for the Cyber Security, Data Science & IoT department, and Coordinator for the Google Developer Group, organizing Capture-the-Flag events at the college level. I hold an M.Tech in Software Engineering from Kakatiya Institute of Technology & Science and a B.Tech in Information Technology from Balaji Institute of Technology & Science, JNTU Hyderabad.
