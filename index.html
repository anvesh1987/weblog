Professional Journey

My teaching career spans over 15 years across five institutions in Telangana.
I began as an Assistant Professor at Kamala Institute of Technology and Science, Karimnagar (2010–2011),
before moving to Ganapathy Engineering College, Warangal (2011–2012).
I then spent over a decade — from 2012 to 2023 — at Jyothishmathi Institute of Technological Sciences, Karimnagar, building my foundation in Computer Science and Cyber Security education.
From March 2023, I joined Malla Reddy College of Engineering & Technology, Kompally, where I taught until June 2026 and took on roles including Placements Coordinator.
I'm currently an Assistant Professor at ACE Engineering College, Ghatkesar, continuing my focus on Cyber Security teaching and research alongside my ongoing Ph.D..
