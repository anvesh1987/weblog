Subtotal — Semester VI12
75

Total distinct Bare Acts / Codes / Sanhitas named across the 3-Year LL.B. Syllabus (Semesters I–VI).
If the Constitution of India (Papers LLB-103 & LLB-203) is also counted, the total becomes 76.

Note: This list counts Bare Acts, Codes and Sanhitas explicitly named (by title) in the syllabus document. A few subjects refer to laws only descriptively without naming them (e.g., law relating to domestic violence, law relating to sexual harassment at workplace, or general references to water/air pollution laws) — these are not counted since the syllabus itself does not name the Act. The Constitution of India is also excluded from the count above since it is a Constitution, not an “Act”; it governs Papers LLB-103 (Constitutional Law-I) and LLB-203 (Constitutional Law-II).
