LLB – Bachelor of Legislative Law

LLB 3-Year Programme (Semester Scheme) · Department of Law, Osmania University
Subject Nomenclature & Semester-wise Importance (W.E.F. Academic Year 2026–2027)

Sem I Sem II Sem III Sem IV Sem V Sem VI
Code Nomenclature Credits Importance
Semester I
LLB-101
Law of Contracts-I
 5
Lays the foundation of contract formation and validity — base of nearly all civil and commercial transactions.
LLB-102
Family Law-I (Hindu Law)
 5
Covers Hindu marriage, joint family and succession principles, core to family-law practice.
LLB-103
Constitutional Law-I
 5
Introduces the structure and fundamental-rights framework of the Constitution — the bedrock of Indian law.
LLB-104
Law of Torts incl. Motor Vehicle Accidents & Consumer Protection
 5
Deals with civil wrongs, compensation claims and consumer rights — highly relevant to litigation practice.
LLB-105
Environmental Law
 5
Addresses pollution control and statutory/common-law environmental remedies, growing in regulatory importance.
Semester II
LLB-201
Law of Contracts-II
 5
Extends contract principles to indemnity, guarantee, bailment and pawn — key for commercial law.
LLB-202
Family Law-II (Muslim & Other Personal Laws)
 5
Covers marriage, dower and succession under Muslim and other personal laws.
LLB-203
Constitutional Law-II
 5
Studies organs of government, centre-state relations and executive powers.
LLB-204
Law of Crimes (Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023)
 5
Covers India's new substantive criminal code — core to criminal law practice.
LLB-205
Law of Evidence (Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, 2023)
 5
Governs relevancy, admissibility and evidentiary value — indispensable for litigation.
Semester III
LLB-301
Jurisprudence
 5
Explores legal theory and sources of law, shaping analytical legal reasoning.
LLB-302
Law of Property
 5
Covers transfer-of-property principles, foundational for property/real-estate practice.
LLB-303
Administrative Law
 5
Studies rule of law and control over executive/administrative action.
LLB-304
Company Law
 5
Covers incorporation, governance documents and corporate management — key for corporate practice.
LLB-305
Labour Law-I
 5
Introduces labour welfare and wage-related legislation.
Semester IV
LLB-401
Labour Law-II
 5
Builds on wage and other labour legislations.
LLB-402
Public International Law
 5
Covers statehood, recognition and succession — relevant for cross-border/diplomatic practice.
LLB-403
Interpretation of Statutes
 5
Teaches rules of statutory construction — a core skill for applying any law.
LLB-404
Land Laws
 5
Covers land reform and tenancy systems, important for agrarian/land law practice.
LLB-405
Intellectual Property Law
 5
Covers international IP conventions, vital in the knowledge economy.
Semester V
LLB-501
Civil Procedure Code & Law of Limitation
 5
Governs procedural rules for civil suits and trials — essential for civil litigation.
LLB-502
Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, Juvenile Justice & Probation of Offenders Act
 5
Covers the new criminal procedure code and juvenile justice framework.
LLB-503
Law of Banking & Negotiable Instruments
 5
Covers banker-customer relations and negotiable instruments — vital for banking/commercial law.
LLB-504
Alternate Dispute Resolution
 5
Covers arbitration, conciliation, mediation and negotiation — increasingly important given court caseloads.
LLB-505
Professional Ethics & Professional Accounting System
 5
Covers advocacy ethics and accounting standards — mandatory for bar-enrollment readiness.
Semester VI
LLB-601
Law of Taxation
 5
Covers Indian taxation laws, relevant for tax practice and advisory.
LLB-602
Information Technology Law
 5
Covers e-governance and cyber-law frameworks, crucial in the digital era.
LLB-603A
Optional (A): Law Relating to Women
 5
Elective specialization in gender-protective statutory law.
LLB-603B
Optional (B): Human Rights Law
 5
Elective specialization in human rights instruments and enforcement.
LLB-603C
Optional (C): Law of Investments & Securities
 5
Elective specialization in securities and investment regulation.
LLB-604
Drafting, Pleadings & Conveyancing
 5
Builds practical drafting skills for legal documents — an essential transactional/litigation skill.
LLB-605
Moot Courts, Observation of Trial, Pre-Trial Prep & Internship
 5
Clinical/practical paper building courtroom advocacy through moots and internships.
